How I work with AI

I use AI tools in every project to research and build faster.

  1. Start on site

    I start where the client works: on a job site or as a guest in the restaurant.

  2. Research

    I use Perplexity for sources, Gemini for comparisons, ChatGPT to structure strategy and Manifold for marketing and social data.

  3. Flow and states

    I list the steps and the states before the build. Example: my brief for this portfolio.

  4. Build

    I build the page or prototype from that list in Claude Code. I make animations in GSAP and HyperFrames, render them to MP4 with Playwright and build 3D in three.js.

  5. Visuals

    I generate images and video in Higgsfield and Leonardo AI, and make posts and materials in Canva.

  6. Review

    Before anything goes to the client, I check it against the brief and the brand.
